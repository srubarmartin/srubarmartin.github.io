## Design Context

### Users
General readers visiting to read blog posts. They should be able to browse and read content effortlessly, with no friction or visual noise getting in the way.

### Brand Personality
**Calm strength** — professional, confident, and clean. The site should feel composed and intentional, never loud or cluttered.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual tone:** Minimal and clean, with a high degree of polish. Every detail should feel considered.
- **Color palette:** Black (or dark grey `#333`) and white (`#ffffff`) as the foundation. Sparse, purposeful use of a single accent color (`#4183C4` blue) for links and interactive elements only — never decorative.
- **Theme:** Light mode only. No dark mode needed.
- **Anti-references:** Nothing flashy, busy, or overly decorated. No gratuitous gradients, shadows, or animations.

### Design Principles
1. **Restraint over decoration** — Remove rather than add. Every element must earn its place.
2. **Typography carries the design** — Clean type hierarchy, generous whitespace, and readable proportions do the heavy lifting.
3. **Quiet confidence** — The design should feel assured and polished without calling attention to itself.
4. **Content first** — Nothing should compete with the writing. Layout, color, and interaction all serve readability.
5. **Polish in the details** — Consistent spacing, aligned elements, smooth transitions, and precise type treatment signal quality without shouting.
